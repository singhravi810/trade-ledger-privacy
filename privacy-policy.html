Trade Ledger Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This policy explains how Trade Ledger handles information when you use the app. Trade Ledger is a personal trading journal. It does not execute trades, act as a broker, or provide investment advice.

Journal information and device storage

Journal entries, goals, language, currency, and related preferences are stored in local app storage on your device. The journal feature does not upload this information to the developer or a developer-operated server. There is no Trade Ledger account or cloud-sync feature. You can clear journal data from the app's settings; uninstalling the app or clearing its storage may also remove it. Exported CSV files are saved where you choose on your device.

Help requests

If you submit a message using Help, the app sends the message and any name or email address you choose to provide to EmailJS so it can deliver the request to Trade Ledger Support. The message is used to respond to you and handle the support issue. Do not include passwords, one-time codes, broker credentials, or other sensitive information.

The app is configured not to save private message content in EmailJS activity history. EmailJS still processes the request to deliver it and may process technical metadata under its own policies. The request is also delivered to the support mailbox, where correspondence is kept while needed to respond and maintain reasonable support records. Copies may remain in email-service backups subject to the provider's retention practices. EmailJS's privacy policy is available at emailjs.com/legal/privacy-policy.

Sharing and advertising

We do not sell personal information. We do not intentionally use advertising or analytics SDKs in the current app. Information is shared with EmailJS only as needed to deliver a Help request, and may be disclosed if required by law or to protect the app and its users.

Retention and your choices

Journal data remains in local app storage until you clear it or remove the app. You control whether to submit a Help request and what optional details to include. To ask about or request deletion of support correspondence, contact us at the address below. Some copies may remain in email systems or backups for a limited period, or where retention is required by law.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled for support. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Protect access to your device and do not send confidential trading or account credentials in a Help request.

Children

Trade Ledger is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The latest version will be published at the public policy URL shown in the app-store listing.

Live market data

The current release does not expose a live market-chart feature. If a future release adds connections to a broker or market-data service, this policy will be updated to explain the data involved before that feature is made available.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Trade Ledger Support at ravi1992@live.in.
